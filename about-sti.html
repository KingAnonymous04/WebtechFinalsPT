The STI History

From its humble beginnings on August 21, 1983 as a computer training center with only two campuses, STI College now has campuses all over the Philippines and has diversified into ICT-enhanced programs in Information Technology, Business and Management, Tourism and Hospitality Management, Engineering, Arts and Sciences, Maritime, and Senior & Junior High Schools.
